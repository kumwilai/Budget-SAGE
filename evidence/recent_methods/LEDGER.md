This is a data-forced substitution of one fixed face landmark by another.
Fill rules (scripts/recent_baselines/slrtp_pt_mapping.py):
- Face fill: the train-split mean face. DESIGN.md Section 4 says "rigidly translated per frame by the predicted neck joint". Because the release fixes the face centroid at the origin in every frame, the translation that keeps the face on its own anchor is zero, so the fill is the constant train-mean face. Translating a centred face by the neck would break the release's own invariant. This is applying the rule in the release's coordinate frame, not a new choice, and it was fixed before scoring.
- Hip fill: neck(t) plus the train-mean offset (hip minus neck). neck is the PT neck joint, which on ground truth is the shoulder midpoint.
Decision rule, copied from Section 4: if the face-plus-hip fill costs at most 1.0 BLEU-4 and at most 0.010 DTW-MJE against the dev identity row, the manual-50 contract stands. Otherwise Sign-IDD moves to full 178. Prediction: BLEU-4 drops 1 to 3, WER rises 2 to 5, DTW rises 0.005 to 0.015.
